# Status

The project has one paper-level publication candidate and several exploratory branches.

- **Publication candidate:** low-multiplicity profiles for zeta zeros.
- **Not yet complete:** external review, Lean integration, and comprehensive priority search.
- **Exploratory only:** xi-prime endpoint transfer, short-interval theorems, depth-sensitive kernels, Nyquist-nullity models, and complementary Hankel observability.
- **Not solved:** the Riemann Hypothesis.

See [`docs/RESEARCH_MAP.md`](docs/RESEARCH_MAP.md) and the full [publication-readiness audit](publication/low-multiplicity-zeta/publication_readiness_audit.md).
