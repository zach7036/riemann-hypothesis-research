# Exploratory RH-adjacent findings

These branches are archived research directions, not established theorems about the Riemann zeta function. Their role is to record useful algebra, obstructions, candidate mechanisms, and unfinished proof programs.

## Amplitude endpoint

A self-similar amplitude formulation appears to remove a finite-section boundary loss at critical bandwidth. The standalone harmonic-analysis estimate and log-energy deweighting are the strongest pieces. A complete `lambda = 1` xi-prime transfer still requires trace assembly, homogeneous re-expansion, endpoint explicit-formula integration, and a full formal build.

## Xi-prime and short intervals

Exact variational calculations locate a candidate simple-zero threshold near `0.51331975984769` for a proposed localized xi-prime method. The numerical/Fredholm optimization is much stronger than the analytic status: a full two-scale local explicit-formula and inertia transfer remains unfinished. A zeta analogue near `0.550193964744...` is likewise only a candidate.

## Depth spectrum

For an isolated reflected off-line pair, the normalized finite-compression spectrum contains a negative eigenvalue whose size records horizontal depth. Abstract cyclic examples show that aggregate spectral moments can erase this information. A positive-definite-kernel argument also indicates a tradeoff between universal positivity and exponential depth amplification. What remains missing is an observable that survives arbitrary zero-side cancellation.

## Critical edge / Nyquist

Smooth vertical progression packets can be nearly invisible at fixed subcritical bandwidth and become resonant in a collar at the critical edge. Interlaced phase classes can create finite-order endpoint nullity. These are model harmonic-analysis statements; no theorem is claimed that actual zeta zeros form or avoid such packets.

## Complementary Hankel detectors

Hankel determinants recover finite phase rank through a Vandermonde factorization, while complementary support accounting can keep a nonlinear product within total prime length `T`. The unresolved step is controlling Cauchy-Binet cancellation and proving an observability/decomposition theorem for arbitrary actual zero clusters.

## Overall RH status

None of these branches currently detects and excludes a single sparse off-line zero pair. The Riemann Hypothesis is not proved. The publication candidate in `publication/low-multiplicity-zeta/` is deliberately separated from these exploratory programs.
