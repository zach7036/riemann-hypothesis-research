# Xi-prime and short-interval findings

**Status: theorem candidates and exact optimization results; not established short-interval theorems.**

The global xi-prime window functional admits a Fredholm/operator formulation. Exact rational certificates developed in this project bracket optimized window values extremely tightly and produce a degree-eight positive profile with candidate simple/on-line proportion about 0.868641500529 and distinct proportion about 0.934320750265.

For localization, the variational calculation gives a candidate xi-prime threshold near 0.51331975984769. A separate zeta short-interval scaling calculation produces a candidate threshold near 0.550193964744. The numerical and variational calculations are much stronger than the present analytic status.

Missing before either short-interval statement becomes a theorem: a complete uniform two-scale explicit formula, local prime moment estimates, entry-dependent re-expansion, local zero-count transfer, final inertia assembly, full formal build, and specialist priority review.
