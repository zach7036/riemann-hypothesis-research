# Notice and provenance

The analytic foundation is the 2026 Claude/Anthropic paper *More than two thirds of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function lie on the critical line* and its public Lean repository `anthropics/zeta-23-lean`. Those external works are not redistributed here and retain their own authorship, copyright, and license.

The low-multiplicity manuscript contributes scalar optimization, multiplicity bookkeeping, exact certificates, and sharpness analysis built on that foundation. It is an unrefereed research draft.

GPT-5.6 Pro and other AI systems assisted with derivation, checking, computation, and writing. Zach Waddle is responsible for deciding what to circulate and for obtaining independent expert validation before representing any new statement as established mathematics.
