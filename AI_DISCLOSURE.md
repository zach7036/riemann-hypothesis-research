# AI assistance disclosure

GPT-5.6 Pro and other AI systems assisted with mathematical exploration, algebraic derivation, adversarial review, code generation, exact-certificate construction, literature-search organization, and manuscript preparation.

The repository deliberately separates:

- a paper-level low-multiplicity theorem candidate;
- exact or computational certificates for narrowly stated functionals;
- exploratory mathematical models and incomplete proof programs.

AI-generated confidence is not treated as verification. New theorem claims require independent specialist review and, where stated, completion of the Lean extension. Zach Waddle controls publication and is responsible for the repository's descriptions and status labels.
