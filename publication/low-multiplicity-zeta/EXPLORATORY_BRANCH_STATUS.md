# Status of the non-manuscript RH branches

This file prevents exploratory calculations from being confused with established zeta theorems.

## Fixed-amplitude finite sections

**Retain:** the standalone finite-section Fourier/Gabor boundary estimate, the log-energy endpoint-deweighting lemma, and the exact fixed-collar arithmetic certificate.

**Missing before a zeta/xi-prime theorem:** a canonical weighted theorem statement, componentwise second-trace assembly, a homogeneous re-expansion implementation, the endpoint explicit-formula family, admissibility instances, a complete pinned build, and independent specialist review.

**Label:** paper-level harmonic-analysis candidate; not an integrated `lambda=1` theorem.

## Short intervals

**Retain:** exact Fredholm/window calculations and the kernel-checked local geometry layer.

**Missing:** two-scale explicit formula, uniform local prime moments, local zero count, entry-dependent re-expansion, final inertia transfer, complete formal build, and priority review.

**Label:** theorem candidate only. The numbers `0.51331975984769...` for `xi'` and `0.550193964744...` for zeta are not established short-interval theorems.

## Depth spectrum and opposed windows

**Retain:** isolated-pair spectral algebra, the abstract aggregate-moment adversary, the positive-definite-kernel decay lemma under its exact hypothesis, and the opposed-window explicit-formula identity.

**Missing:** a noncancelling observability theorem for arbitrary actual zero configurations.

**Label:** exploratory harmonic analysis; no density theorem.

## Critical-edge / Nyquist analysis

**Retain:** Poisson invisibility below the critical edge, endpoint resonance, transition-width calculation, and finite-order nullity scaling in the model packet setting.

**Missing:** any theorem excluding or controlling Nyquist-null configurations of actual zeta zeros.

**Label:** model theorem / obstruction, not a zeta-zero theorem.

## Complementary-band Hankel determinants

**Retain:** Hankel--Vandermonde factorization, Cauchy--Binet expansion, and complementary support-budget algebra.

**Missing:** control of cancellation among subset terms, archimedean and convolution bookkeeping at theorem precision, a phase-rank decomposition of arbitrary clusters, and an unconditional observability inequality.

**Label:** research program; structured-packet conclusions depend on explicit stability assumptions.

## Riemann Hypothesis

No branch supplies a mechanism that detects and excludes a single sparse off-line pair. RH is not proved, and none of the exploratory packet estimates should be described as a proof of the Density Hypothesis or a general zero-density improvement.
